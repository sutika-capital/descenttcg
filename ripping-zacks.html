Our story

Where the rips happen

Long before Descent TCG had a storefront, Ripping Zacks was the name collectors knew — ripping packs, chasing grails, and sharing every pull with the community. What started as one collector's obsession with the hobby became a following of people who love the rip as much as the card.

Today, Ripping Zacks lives on as the community and content brand behind Descent TCG. The shop in Bradenton is where the cards live; Ripping Zacks is where the fun happens — pack rips, box breaks, chase-card hunts, and the big pulls, all documented as they happen.
