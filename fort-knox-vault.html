Fort Knox Vault

Updated live via Collectr.

The Fort Knox Vault is Descent TCG's live high-end sealed inventory — the booster boxes, sealed cases, and Elite Trainer Boxes we've put away, straight from our collector-run shop at 5205 33rd St E, Bradenton, FL. Everything below is powered by Collectr and updated as pieces come in and sell, so what you see is what's actually on the shelf right now.

What's in the vault

A rotating stock of sealed Pokémon and Magic: The Gathering product, including:

  • Pokémon booster boxes and booster cases
  • Elite Trainer Boxes and Pokémon Center exclusives
  • Magic: The Gathering booster boxes and collector boosters
  • Special-edition and holiday sealed sets

How to buy

Sealed inventory moves fast — boxes and cases can sell between updates. For current pricing and availability on anything in the vault, reach out and we'll confirm what's still sealed on the shelf.
